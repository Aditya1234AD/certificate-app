Privacy Policy

1. Information Collected

We collect personal details such as name, mobile number, address, certificate type, and uploaded documents (Aadhaar, ROR, applicant photo, father’s Aadhaar). Payment information is securely handled via Razorpay; we do not store card details.

2. Use of Information

Your information is used to process applications, provide status updates, and generate certificates after successful payment.

3. Data Sharing

Information is stored securely using Supabase and shared only with authorized personnel for processing. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

4. Security

We implement encryption and strict access controls to protect your personal data and documents.

5. Razorpay Payments

All payments are securely processed via Razorpay. Card and bank details are never stored on our servers.

6. User Rights

You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data by contacting the admin.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated periodically. Users will be notified of significant changes.
