Razorpay Payment Policy

1. Payment Gateway

All payments on this portal are processed using Razorpay, a secure and trusted payment gateway. We do not store card or bank details on our servers.

2. Secure Transactions

Razorpay uses industry-standard security protocols, including SSL encryption, to ensure that all transactions are secure and confidential.

3. User Responsibility

Users must provide accurate payment details and ensure sufficient balance in their accounts before making a transaction. The portal is not responsible for failed transactions due to user error or network issues.

4. Payment Confirmation

Once a payment is successfully completed, it will be reflected in your application status as Paid. In case of any delay, the status may take a few minutes to update.

5. Refunds & Disputes

All payment disputes, refunds, or chargebacks must be handled via Razorpay’s support channels according to their terms. The portal can assist but cannot override Razorpay policies.

6. Compliance

By making a payment through Razorpay, you agree to comply with Razorpay’s Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.
